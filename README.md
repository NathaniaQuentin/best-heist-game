The Gilded Hour — a top-down museum heist game. Mara Voss slips into the Meridian Museum after dark to steal the Sun Ruby from its glass case and reach the service door unseen. Three guards patrol with vision cones, security cameras sweep the hall, and the alarm meter climbs every time she is spotted. Coins distract guards; side loot is worth extra. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best heist game” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server --directory game 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD or arrows move, Shift sneaks, click tosses a coin to distract a guard. Crack the ruby case by standing beside it, grab side loot on the way, and get back to the service door before the alarm hits 100.
